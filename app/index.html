    Roadmap to the Platinum

    Play the story first, then the strangers, then collectibles, then activities and money — the platinum pops with the last cleanup trophy. Built from the official descriptions: which chapters and missions, which collectibles and counts, in what order.

    Missable trophies

    Only 2 of 55 trophies are flagged missable. Both are one-shot moments tied to a single place and event in the official descriptions — do them the first time the game gives you the chance. Every other trophy can be earned in free roam after the story, and every story trophy unlocks by finishing its chapter or mission.

      About & sources

      Leonida Legend is an offline guide and tracker for the 55 PlayStation 5 trophies of Grand Theft Auto VI: 1 platinum, 2 gold, 6 silver and 46 bronze. It runs entirely from its own files — no network requests, no account, no tracking.

      Where the facts come from

      • Official trophy names, descriptions and grades: the official list on Exophase — exophase.com/game/grand-theft-auto-vi-psn/trophies/
      • PSN progress counts (where a trophy tracks one): PSNProfiles — psnprofiles.com/trophies/43531-grand-theft-auto-vi
      • Every guide is written in our own words for this entry, from the official descriptions. A guide that repeats a trophy's description adds nothing, so each one says what to do, where and how, and when it can be missed — and names its sources beside it.

      How the roadmap was built

      The roadmap and the missable flags are built from the official descriptions alone: story chapters and missions first, then stranger strands, then collectibles with their counts, then activities and money. No location is pinned down until it is checked in the game — where a place is not confirmed, the guide says so instead of inventing one.

      Your progress

      Progress is stored in this browser's localStorage under the key gta6-trophy-guide-v1. Export writes it to a JSON file you can keep or move to another browser; Import reads such a file back. Nothing leaves your machine.

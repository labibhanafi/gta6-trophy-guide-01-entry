      Missable trophies

      Only one trophy in the whole list can be missed. Everything else is either story-unmissable or doable in free roam after the credits — there is no mission replay, so the one missable step matters.

      Safe by design

      • All story trophies (chapters, missions, finale) — unmissable, they pop on completion.
      • All collectibles & activities — free roam continues after the story, so nothing is locked out.
      • No online trophies — all 55 are single-player and offline.

      About this guide

      Leonida Legend is an offline, open-source guide and tracker for the 55 PlayStation 5 trophies of Grand Theft Auto VI (1 platinum, 2 gold, 6 silver, 46 bronze; 28 hidden). It runs entirely from its own files — no network, no account, no ads.

      How the guides are written

      Every guide is written in our own words for this entry, from the official trophy descriptions. Where a guide gives a specific location, count or step, it names its source beside it — PowerPyx's trophy guide & roadmap, the Exophase trophy list, and PSNProfiles for PSN's counts. Where the early guides have not yet pinned a detail down, the guide says so plainly instead of inventing it.

      Sources

      • PowerPyx — GTA 6 Trophy Guide & Roadmap (difficulty 4/10, ~200 h, missable flag, roadmap steps)
      • Exophase — GTA VI trophy list (official names, descriptions, grades)
      • PSNProfiles — GTA VI trophies (grades and PSN progress counts)

      Your progress

      Progress is saved in this browser only (localStorage). Export downloads a JSON file; Import restores it. Nothing leaves your machine.

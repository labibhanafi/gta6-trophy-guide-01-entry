Road to the platinum

  1. Step 1 — Play the story. Chapters 1–4 and the final mission, plus the named story missions. Nothing here can be missed: missions replay.
  2. Step 2 — Stranger strands. Finish all 8 stranger mission strands across Leonida, including the final missions for Andy, Kenton, Billy, Randy and Tyrone.
  3. Step 3 — Explore & collect. All 52 areas, 18 meditation spots, card sets, 100 WAiNK It locations, 100 vehicles and the garage properties.
  4. Step 4 — Activities & grind. Races, the shooting range, stunts, challenges, bank vaults, gang operations and the money trophies.
  5. Step 5 — Cleanup & platinum. 100% completion for “A Long Road”, then the platinum “Vice Got a Price!” pops.

Do the missable trophies in Step 1 while their moments are live — they are flagged MISSABLE below.

Roadmap to the platinum

Built from the official trophy descriptions. Four stages, in order — story first, then the world, then the clean-up.

  1. Stage 1 — Story first

    Play Chapters 1–4 and the final mission, plus the contact strands for Andy, Kenton, Billy, Randy and Tyrone. Every chapter and mission trophy here is unmissable.

  2. Stage 2 — Activities & side content

    Stranger strands, races, the Shooting Range, fishing, arcade games, challenges and the partner moments. Do the missable ones now — they are flagged below.

  3. Stage 3 — Collectibles & the map

    Card sets, WAiNK It spots, meditation spots, vehicles, all 52 areas, gang operations, garages and Scooter Bros registrations. Sweep region by region.

  4. Stage 4 — 100% & the platinum

    Finish the 100% completion checklist for A Long Road, then the platinum Vice Got a Price! pops on its own.

Missable trophies — do these in time

These need a specific moment, partner or set-up. The rest of the list can be cleaned up after the story.
